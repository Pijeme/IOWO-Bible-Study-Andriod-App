Welcome · Use and privacy agreement

This app is free for authorized IOWO accounts. It includes Bible texts and study resources for reading, personal study and ministry preparation.

Please respect the publishers' copyright notices and attributions shown with each resource. Permission to use the app does not transfer ownership of those resources.

Your notes, highlights, bookmarks and reading preferences are saved on your device. Optional online synchronization sends your sermon notes through the IOWO website to the configured Google Drive backup. The shared installation contains no other users' notes or account passwords.

Your existing website credentials are sent over HTTPS for the first login. The app securely remembers a revocable device sign-in; it does not retain your password. Normal offline use does not require repeated login.

There are no advertisements, subscriptions or paid upgrades. Export or sync important notes before uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

You control whether to start online backup. Publisher and contact details are available from About Publisher on the loading screen.
